# Data source

- **File:** `firas_monopole_spec_v1.txt` (raw, unchanged), converted to `firas.csv` by `prepare.py` (same numbers, Fermium header).
- **URL:** https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/cobe/firas/monopole_spec/firas_monopole_spec_v1.txt
- **Downloaded:** 2026-09-27 (curl, HTTP 200).
- **Publisher:** NASA LAMBDA (Legacy Archive for Microwave Background Data Analysis), COBE/FIRAS products.
- **Citation:** D. J. Fixsen, E. S. Cheng, J. M. Gales, J. C. Mather, R. A. Shafer, E. L. Wright,
  "The Cosmic Microwave Background Spectrum from the Full COBE FIRAS Data Set", ApJ **473**, 576 (1996), Table 4;
  D. J. Fixsen & J. C. Mather, ApJ **581**, 817 (2002) (the 2.725 K scale used to build column 2).
- **License:** NASA data, public domain (US Government work); LAMBDA asks that the original papers be cited.
