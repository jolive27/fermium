# Data source

- **Files:** `nubase_excerpt.txt` (the 128 NUBASE2020 ground-state lines used: parents, daughters and ⁴He, verbatim) and
  `alpha.csv` (103 even–even α emitters with Q, half-life, α branch), written by `prepare.py` from the full table.
- **URL:** https://www-nds.iaea.org/amdc/ame2020/nubase_4.mas20.txt
- **Downloaded:** 2026-09-27 (curl, HTTP 200; `prepare.py` downloads it again if run).
- **Publisher:** IAEA Atomic Mass Data Center (AMDC).
- **Citation:** F. G. Kondev, M. Wang, W. J. Huang, S. Naimi and G. Audi, "The NUBASE2020 evaluation of nuclear physics
  properties", Chinese Physics C **45**, 030001 (2021).
- **License:** freely available from the AMDC; cite the paper.
- **Published formula compared:** A. Sobiczewski, Z. Patyk and S. Ćwiok, Phys. Lett. B **224**, 1 (1989): the Viola–Seaborg
  coefficients a = 1.66175, b = −8.5166, c = −0.20228, d = −33.9069 (typed from the paper, not downloaded).
